- Hourly sales (Hourly tab, Ask tool `hourly_profile`) read the `Hourly Sales` tab of the Sysco and Daily Numbers workbook (columns Date yyyy-mm-dd, Store, Hour as 24h start of hour so 7 means 7:00 to 8:00 AM, Net Sales, Checks). Same workbook ID as Sysco, already in the allowlist. Rules for the data:
  1. Only hours with activity have rows. A missing hour for a store and date is zero sales, not missing data.
  2. Checks is filled only for Massillon. For Ashland and Wadsworth the cell is blank because their source gives item quantity, not checks. A blank is never zero, and checks or average check are never shown for Ashland or Wadsworth (see `HOURLY_CHECK_STORES` in `src/engine2.js`).
  3. Ashland and Wadsworth hourly Net Sales comes from the POS Sales Mix report. A day's hourly sum does not equal the Daily Log Net Sales (it runs roughly minus $112 to plus $89 a day). Use it only as a within day distribution: shape, peak hour, percent of day. Never reconcile, overwrite or flag Daily Log figures against hourly sums.
  4. Massillon hourly sums tie exactly to its Daily Log Net Sales and its Checks are real, so average check is fine for Massillon only.
  5. Known harmless quirk: Massillon 2026-10-29 hourly checks sum to 134 while the source report prints 133. The rows are correct as loaded.
  6. Ashland and Wadsworth get a new day appended every morning. Massillon is updated by hand in batches, so its latest date can lag. The tab defaults to each store's own latest date and says how far behind it is, and never treats that as an error.
  7. Hourly data is never shown from a snapshot, the tab says it is not connected until Google answers. The test rows live in `test/hourly.fixture.json` and are sample data used only by `test/server.js`.
- Stock health (Orders tab, pill Stock health, `src/burn.js`) reads a `Burn Rate` tab in each of the Enhancer Order / Inventory workbook and the Cookie Order / Inventory workbook (both already in the allowlist). Columns: Store, Item (headed Enhancer or Cookie), Last Count (yyyy-mm-dd), Stock On Hand, Used Last Interval, Burn per week, Company Burn per week (all stores combined), Weeks of Cover, Projected Run-Out (yyyy-mm-dd), Notes. Units are tubs for enhancers and individual cookies for cookies. Rules for the data:
  1. Pacer maintains these tabs and they update on the count cycle, about weekly, when the store inventory counts are processed. It is not real time. Last Count is the freshness stamp per store and stores can be on different dates, so the count date is shown wherever burn or cover is shown.
  2. A blank Burn, Weeks of Cover or Projected Run-Out means not computable yet, never zero. The Notes column says why (one count only, no counts yet, or a bulk delivery whose split between stores was never recorded). Those rows render as a calm "Not enough counts yet" state, not an error and not zero burn. A Stock On Hand of 0 is a real zero and shows as Out of stock.
  3. Cookie burn is mostly empty by design: Ashland has a baseline count only, Wadsworth and Massillon have no counts yet. A store with no counts shows one calm card instead of empty rows.
  4. Whey is the most complete series and its per store burns sum to Company Burn. Company burn is secondary context, shown in a muted last column.
  5. Rows sort tightest first: out of stock, then weeks of cover ascending, then rows not computable yet in sheet order. Anything under two weeks of cover is flagged and listed under Needs attention.
  6. Notes shown on screen have hyphens turned into words (see `bnote`). Nothing writes back, and there is no snapshot, the view says it is not connected until Google answers. Test rows live in `test/burn.fixture.json` (copied from the live tabs on 2026-11-11, plus one sample Ashland soy row at 1.9 weeks so the flag can be tested) and are used only by `test/server.js`.
  7. The Supply tab still computes its own enhancer burn from the Enhancer Store Log, so its numbers can differ from Pacer's Burn Rate tab.
- Usage intensity (Supply tab Enhancers table, `computeStoreBurn` and `enhIntensity` in `src/engine2.js`, Ask tool `enhancer_history`) is enhancer tubs used per $1,000 of Daily Log net sales, per store. Rules:
  1. It uses the exact same clean cycles as the store burn (up to the last three count to count cycles). Tubs used over those cycles divided by that store's Daily Log net sales over the same dates, times 1,000. The sales span for a cycle is the day after the earlier count date through the later count date, inclusive. Cycles are pooled, not averaged.
  2. It is never estimated. If any sales day in the span is missing, or there is no burn for the store, intensity is blank and shows the calm "Not enough counts yet" state. A zero use cycle is a real zero.
  3. Company intensity is total tubs used over total sales for the stores that have intensity.
  4. A store is marked Worth a look when its intensity is at least 1.5 times the lowest store on the same item. Only stores that used at least 1 tub over their cycles are compared, because counts are in quarter tubs. It needs two such stores. It is a comparison, not an accusation, and is never converted to a cost.
  5. Sales are Daily Log net sales only. No payroll or pay data. Cookies are not included yet.
